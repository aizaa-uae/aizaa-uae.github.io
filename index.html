Which free zone fits your business?

Price is rarely the deciding factor. Start from what your business actually does, then compare cost inside that shortlist.

Solo founder or consultant on a budget

You need a licence, a visa and a low renewal bill. Flexi-desk is enough.

Shortlist: SHAMS, SPC, RAKEZ, Ajman FZ, UAQ FTZ

You want a Dubai address

A Dubai-issued licence can help with client perception and some banks, at roughly double the northern-emirate price.

Shortlist: Meydan, IFZA, Dubai South

Trading, commodities or a premium brand

Higher cost and a mandatory JLT workspace, in exchange for strong recognition with banks and counterparties.

Shortlist: DMCC

Warehousing, logistics or manufacturing

You need real space: warehouses, land or port access. Office rules are stricter but the infrastructure is there.

Shortlist: JAFZA, RAKEZ, Dubai South, Ajman FZ

Software, IT or tech startup

Technology-focused campus with co-working and an ecosystem of tech companies around you.

Shortlist: Dubai Silicon Oasis (Dtec), IFZA, Meydan

Financial services or holding structures

Independent English common-law courts and financial regulators. The most expensive option, and worth it only if you need that framework.

Shortlist: DIFC, ADGM
Selling mainly to UAE customers? A free zone company generally cannot trade directly on the UAE mainland without a distributor or extra permits. If most of your clients are local, compare a mainland licence before you commit.

The costs the headline price leaves out

The cheapest advertised licence almost always has zero visas. These are the typical extras to add per year when you want UAE residency.

  • Residence visa processing (in-country, per person)~AED 4,000–5,000
  • Establishment card (company immigration file)~AED 1,500 / yr
  • Immigration card~AED 1,500 / yr
  • Medical test and Emirates ID~AED 700–800
  • Mandatory health insurancefrom ~AED 320 / yr
  • Extra activities or shareholders beyond the packagevaries by zone
  • Office upgrade to unlock more visasAED 15,000+ / yr
  • Annual audit (e.g. DMCC, JAFZA)auditor's fee
Ask for the renewal price in writing. First-year discounts are common, and the year-two bill is where budgets break. Compare zones at the same visa count, office type and activity set.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest free zone in the UAE in 2026?

On headline licence price, the northern-emirate and Sharjah zones lead: UAQ FTZ, Ajman Free Zone, RAKEZ, SHAMS and SPC Free Zone all start at roughly AED 5,000 to 6,000 for a zero-visa licence. Once you add a residence visa, expect roughly AED 14,000 to 17,000 in year one.

Does a zero-visa licence give me UAE residency?

No. A zero-visa package gives you a company and a trade licence, but no residence visa. You need a package with at least one visa allocation, and then pay the visa processing, medical, Emirates ID and insurance costs on top.

Which free zones require a physical office?

DMCC requires a workspace in its JLT towers, JAFZA requires a leased office or warehouse, and DIFC requires office space inside DIFC. Zones such as Meydan, IFZA, SHAMS and SPC include a flexi-desk in their packages.

How much does one free zone residence visa cost?

As a planning figure, budget around AED 4,000 to 5,000 per visa for an in-country application, plus an establishment card and immigration card of roughly AED 1,500 each per year, plus mandatory health insurance.

Can I set up a free zone company without visiting the UAE?

Many zones, including Meydan, IFZA, SHAMS, SPC and RAKEZ, let you incorporate fully online. You will still need to be in the UAE for the visa medical and Emirates ID biometrics if you want residency, and most banks will want to meet you before opening an account.
